Mixamo terms: summary (NOT a verbatim snapshot)

As published in the Mixamo FAQ:
- Mixamo characters and animations may be used royalty-free in personal, commercial and
  non-profit projects, including creating and distributing video games.
- Use requires an Adobe ID; the account owner (the Fr33World owner) downloaded the clips.
- The raw Mixamo files may not be redistributed or resold on their own (for example as an
  asset pack). They may only ship embedded in a project.

How Fr33World complies:
- Raw FBX downloads stay outside git (.raw/, the owner's Drive).
- Only a converted, optimised, game-specific pack (assets/runtime/animations/) ships,
  loaded by the game client.
