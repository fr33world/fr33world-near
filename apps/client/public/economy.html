The city's economy

The game sells everything for its own $. What players buy to keep is theirs on NEAR: every car bought at the dealership is minted to the buyer's own wallet. Players sell cars to each other for $ or USDC, and the buyer chooses. Sol Motors, the dealership, runs itself: its wallet gets 5% of every USDC sale and the $ top-ups, and its AI buys cars listed far under their worth and sells them again, within limits written into its contract. All USDC comes from players. Balances are read from the chain. This is testnet: test USDC has no real value.
