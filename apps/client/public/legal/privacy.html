Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-06

What we keep

  • Your login account id from Privy, and the name you play under.
  • Your date of birth, only to check that you're 18 or older.
  • Game data: your in-game money and its history, your cars, your position and actions while you play.
  • The address of the wallet created for your account, for USDC.
  • Basic technical data (IP address, device and browser type) to run and protect the game.

What we don't keep

Voice is sent live to the players near you and isn't recorded. We don't sell your data.

Who else handles it

Privy (login and wallets), our hosting providers (Render, Cloudflare), LiveKit (voice), and the NEAR network (payments and wallet addresses are public on the blockchain).

Your choices

You can ask us to show or delete your data at fr33worldgame@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your game data; blockchain records can't be erased.
