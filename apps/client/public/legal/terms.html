Terms of Play

Version 2026-11-06

Who can play

fr33world is for adults: you must be 18 or older. We ask your date of birth once and an account that gives an age under 18 can't play.

Your account

You log in through our login provider (Privy) with email, Google, Discord or X, or as a guest. Guest progress is kept on one device and may be lost. Keep your login to yourself; one person per account.

In-game money

The game's $ exists only inside fr33world. It has no cash value, can't be bought or sold for real money, and can't be withdrawn. We may correct balances that came from bugs, cheating or exploits.

USDC

Your account can have a wallet on the NEAR blockchain, created for you through Privy. Its key is yours: we can't move what's in it. USDC is separate from the game's $, and one can't be turned into the other. Players pay each other through the game's businesses for goods and deliveries; we add no money of our own and promise no earnings. The game currently runs on NEAR's test network, where test USDC has no value.

Playing fair and being decent

No cheating, hacking, exploiting bugs, botting or selling accounts. No harassment, hate, threats or sexual content involving anyone, and nothing illegal, in text or voice. We may suspend or close accounts that break these rules.

Voice

Proximity voice lets players near you hear you while you talk. Be respectful; you can mute others. We don't record voice.

The game changes

fr33world is new: things will change, break or be reset, including progress and balances. Questions or problems: fr33worldgame@gmail.com.
